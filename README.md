# ShopNest E-Commerce Dashboard

## Overview
A comprehensive Power BI dashboard built on 9 interlinked datasets from ShopNest, a Portuguese e-commerce marketplace. Answers 8 key business questions through interactive visuals and DAX measures.

## Datasets Used
- Orders, Customers, Products, Sellers, Payments, Reviews, Geolocation, Order Items, Product Categories

## Business Questions Answered
1. Top product categories by total sales
2. Number of delayed orders per category
3. Monthly comparison of delayed vs on-time orders
4. Most frequently used payment methods
5. Top 10 highest and bottom 10 lowest rated products
6. States with highest and lowest sales
7. Seasonal and quarterly sales patterns
8. Total revenue trends over time (yearly)

## Tools Used
- Power BI, DAX, Data Modelling

## Key Findings
- ~8% of orders were delayed
- Credit card was the dominant payment method (73%)
- Bed/bath/table was the highest revenue category

## Key Skills Demonstrated
- Data modelling across 9 tables
- DAX measures for calculated columns and KPIs
- Drillthrough cross-report analysis
- Dynamic slicers and interactive visuals
- Time-series and geographical analysis
